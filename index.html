With /Glow

Light check

Caps brightness and slows every change so nothing strobes. Turn it on after sundown, or all the time for anyone sensitive to flashing light.

Music you can see.

/Glow listens through your phone and runs the music through effects pedals, like a guitar rig for your lights. Stomp a footswitch, turn a knob, save the board as a rig. Every look you make is yours, so no two rooms glow the same.

Mood mode

Hold the power button. The room follows along.

Mood listens to what’s playing and changes your lights for you. No timeline to program, nothing to sync: pause, skip, shuffle, start late, it keeps up. Start it from the /Glow tile in your phone’s pull-down shade, no app needed.

Music mood

Put a playlist on shuffle. When the next song starts, /Glow listens for a few seconds and moves to the look from your rotation that fits it: calm looks for slow songs, big ones for the bangers, deeper colors for darker tracks.

/Glow locks onto the kick drum and shows the tempo; Half time calms the fast songs. Pick the looks in the rotation, or let it use them all.

Movie mode

The soundtrack drives the room: dark and still in the quiet stretches, calm through dialogue, big when the score swells, and a flash on every jump scare. Low thuds can stutter the lights, footstep by footstep.

Type a movie title and the AI designs its moods from that film’s world.

Questions

Do I need new hardware, rewiring or flashing?

No. /Glow talks to the controller you already have, the same way its original app does.

Can I run several rooms or places?

Yes. Each place is a location with its own controllers, zones and looks: your car, the TV wall, the holiday lights. /Glow connects to one location at a time.

Does it work with Google Home or Apple Home?

Not yet. /Glow runs on your phone and talks to the lights directly. Smart-home support is on the roadmap.

Is it safe for people sensitive to flashing lights?

Turn on Always gentle in Settings. It caps brightness and slows every change so no look, scene or movie scare can strobe. Effects running inside a controller itself can only be dimmed, so stick to /Glow’s own looks.

Can I use it in my shop or restaurant?

Yes. A spare Android phone near the controllers can run your lights all day, with a day look and an after-hours look.

What does it cost?

The core app is free. Pro is a one-time $9.99 unlock for extras like Day → Night, music-reactive patterns, rigs on a zone and full pedal chains, and it works with your own AI key. No key? /Glow AI is a $9.99/year add-on with no setup. Beta testers get both at 50% off, for life.
