What you get

Everything the stock app should have done.

Accurate color

Color correction and per-zone calibration, so the dash and the footwells show the same lilac you picked. Each zone gets its own brightness, too.

Looks you can shape

Breathe, fade, bursts, heartbeat, or build your own with LFO modulators. Give each zone its own effect and speed, or let one zone dance to a rig.

Help that knows your setup

Ask why something looks off and it reads your actual settings. Connect your own AI key and it builds looks, rigs and welcome scenes, and tunes a zone’s color with a 15-second try.

Day → Night

Your day look blends into a calmer night look every evening, and back in the morning. No tapping required.

Welcome & goodbye

A scene as the lights come on and another as they switch off. Pick Materialize, Ignite or Slow sunrise, or describe your own and the AI writes it.

Wakes with your car

When your phone joins your car’s Bluetooth, /Glow reconnects and plays your welcome, even with the app fully closed. Keeps running with the screen off. Share the car? When another phone is running the lights, /Glow shows them as in use instead of fighting over them.

Mood mode

Hold the power button and your lights follow the playlist: each new song gets the look that fits it, locked to the kick drum. Parked and watching something? Movie mode goes dark in the quiet parts and flashes on the scares. Lights, Mood and Day → Night are also in your pull-down shade.

Gentle at night

From sunset to sunrise, /Glow caps brightness and slows every change, so nothing flashes in your eyes on a dark road. Or keep it gentle all the time.

Set up in three steps.

  1. 1

    Close the old app

    Light controllers accept one app at a time. Force-stop the one that came with your kit.

  2. 2

    Connect in /Glow

    Sit in the car with the lights powered and tap Connect. /Glow detects the controller and sets up your zones.

  3. 3

    Pick a look

    Choose a starting look and a night mode, then link your car’s Bluetooth so the welcome plays as you get in.

Will it work with my lights?

/Glow works with the Bluetooth controllers used by many aftermarket ambient light kits, in any car or truck: interiors, footwells, underglow, bed and rock lights. If your kit’s app shows a controller named like one of these, /Glow can most likely drive it:

LEDCAR LEDDMX LEDBLE ELK-BLEDOM SP110E · SP6xxE

Not sure? Join the beta and tell us your kit. Testing new controllers is exactly what the beta is for.

Private by design

  • No account, no tracking.
  • Music is analyzed on the phone and never recorded or sent.
  • Speed glow uses GPS speed in the moment; it’s never stored.
  • Waking with your car only notices your phone joining the car’s Bluetooth. /Glow never talks to the car.
  • An optional AI key stays encrypted on your phone and only goes to the provider you chose.

Questions

Do I need to rewire or flash anything?

No. /Glow talks to the controller you already have, the same way its original app does.

Can it control my car’s built-in ambient lighting?

No. /Glow is for aftermarket Bluetooth kits. Factory lighting is controlled by the car itself.

What does it cost?

The core app is free. Pro is a one-time $9.99 unlock for extras like Mood, Album mode, Day → Night, music-reactive looks and full pedal chains, and it works with your own AI key. No key? /Glow AI is a $9.99/year add-on with no setup. During the beta, Pro is free for testers, and beta testers keep 50% off both, for life, once purchases open.

Does waking with my car work with any car?

Any car that connects to your phone over Bluetooth for calls and music. How soon it fires depends on when your car connects: some do it as the door opens, others once the screen boots.

Will the lights distract me at night?

Turn on Gentle at night: after sunset /Glow caps brightness and slows every change, so music and looks stay soft and nothing strobes. Always gentle does the same all day, for anyone sensitive to flashing light.
