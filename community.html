How to share

Share yours.

Every look, rig and scene saves as a small file. Tag it with the place it’s for, so the person with the same TV wall or storefront finds it.

PRO marks a share that uses at least one Pro feature. Sharing and importing are always free, so you can try any of them.

  1. 1In /Glow, open the look, rig or scene and tap Share.
  2. 2Pick the kind of place it’s for: car or truck, home, entertainment center, holiday, cabinets, display case, man cave or bar, storefront, or restaurant.
  3. 3Post it to the gallery or r/[YOUR_SUBREDDIT]. Anyone can open it in Settings in two taps.
