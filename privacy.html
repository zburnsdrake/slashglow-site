Privacy policy

/Glow: Light Studio, the Android app and slashglow.com. Made by ORX Design (ORX Strategy LLC). Last updated November 17, 2026.

The short version: /Glow runs on your phone. Your lights, music, looks and listening history stay there. /Glow has no accounts and no analytics, and sells nothing about you. A few features you choose to use send specific things to services you pick; each one is listed below.

What stays on your phone

  • Your lights and settings: controllers, zones, locations, looks, rigs, scenes and schedules are stored in the app on your phone.
  • Sound: Music mode, Mood and Movie mode listen through the microphone. The sound is analyzed on the phone as it plays. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • What's playing: with Follow your music app on, Android lets /Glow see the song, artist, album and playing state of the music app on your phone, and the colors of its album art (worked out on the phone). /Glow also reads Pixel's Now Playing notification and no other app's notifications. Looks you pick are remembered per song, album or artist on the phone.
  • Listening history you import: files you choose (for example a Spotify or Last.fm export) are read and counted on the phone and not kept.
  • Location: only for features that need it (for example Speed glow), used on the phone and kept to roughly whole degrees where it is stored.
  • Logs: the connection log stays on the phone and leaves out keys, controller addresses, song titles and locations. It goes somewhere only if you choose to send it.

Your AI key is stored in Android's secure storage, only ever sent to the provider it belongs to, and never included in backups, logs or exports.

The website and its planner

  • slashglow.com is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no analytics or ads.
  • The planner chat sends your messages to Google's Gemini through our server to get a reply. Your messages are not stored or logged by /Glow. The planner uses Gemini's paid tier, under which Google doesn't use what you type to improve its products. To keep the free planner from being abused, the server counts messages per browser and per network address each day; addresses are kept only as a scrambled code that changes daily, and counts are deleted after two days. A Cloudflare check confirms you're not a bot.
  • Sign-up forms are run by the form service linked from the button, and what you enter there is used only to send you the beta invite or the iPhone news.
  • Some parts links are affiliate links, labeled as paid links. /Glow may earn a commission if you buy; the store sets its own cookies under its own policy.

Children

/Glow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Every feature that sends something is off until you turn it on, and can be turned off in the app. Removing your AI key stops all AI requests. Uninstalling /Glow, or clearing its storage, deletes everything it kept on the phone.
