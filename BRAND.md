# /Glow brand guide

## Type
- **Inter, everywhere.** Headings, body, buttons, the logo: one typeface. Weights 400, 500, 600 and 700 are loaded from Google Fonts.
- **Never use Space Grotesk**, in any heading, logo, mockup, image or app screen. It was tried and rejected in Nov 2026: its squared-off "g" reads as cut off, and the headings didn't feel like /Glow.
- Headings: Inter 700, slightly tight (letter-spacing about -0.5px at 28–32px, more negative on the big hero headline).
- In the Android app, the system font (Roboto) is fine; don't load a display font there.

## Logo
- The wordmark is `/glow`: the slash in accent blue `#8ea6ff`, "glow" in `#f2f4f8`, Inter 700, letter-spacing -0.5px.
- The product's full name is "/Glow: Light Studio"; in running text write "/Glow".

## Voice
- Plain and short. Say what it does, not how amazing it is.
- Don't promise features that aren't built: label them with their milestone ("coming soon", "in testing").
- "Paid link" next to every affiliate link.
